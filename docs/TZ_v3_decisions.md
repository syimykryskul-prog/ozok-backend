# OZOK v3 — обязательные проектные решения (ревью Opus QA, 15.11.2026)

Эти решения **обязательны** при реализации `TZ_v3_plan.md` и имеют приоритет над ним. Таблицы ниже — из QA-ревью; колонка «Proposed resolution» = принятое решение.

**РЕШЕНИЕ ЗАКАЗЧИКА 15.11.2026: вся система работает ТОЛЬКО В СОМАХ (KGS).** Никаких USD, курсов валют, `ExchangeRate`, `Contract.currency`, конвертаций. Суммы из ТЗ NBS в долларах задаются в Настройках в сомах: франшиза «Сразу» (по умолчанию 87 500 сом ≈ $1 000), «Рассрочка до» (175 000 сом ≈ $2 000), вознаграждение рекрутеру (по умолчанию 13 125 сом ≈ $150) — всё редактируется. Пункт C1 (смешение валют) снимается; пункт «курсы валют» в P0/P3 — удалить.

Решения оркестратора по пунктам, где был выбор (значения по умолчанию, меняются в Настройках P0):
- **H1:** ставка (Standard/Growth, лид NBS) берётся на дату операции `paid_at` (дата банковской операции, не импорта); многомесячная оплата НЕ дробится по месяцам. Настройка `split_prepaid_by_month = false` — задел.
- **C10:** установка OnAI продана дешевле 20 000 → доли делятся пропорционально (12/20 и 8/20). Доход франчайзи по установке с SetupSplitRule = partner-часть; удержания и доля NBS по лидам NBS считаются от неё.
- **C4:** `verified_by ≠ paid_by` обязательно для выплат (комиссии, рекрутеры, внедренцы).
- **H9:** `recruited_by` — любой сотрудник NBS; вознаграждение создаётся, если у пользователя роль recruiter или флаг `recruiter_reward_enabled`.
- **M9:** блок «Маркетинг» в рейтинге для Standard — нейтральные 50% (настройка).
- **M11 (роль финансиста):** finance может менять курсы, проверять/проводить выплаты, вводить/импортировать оплаты; правила комиссий и цены меняют super_admin (и network_head — только чтение). До подтверждения NBS.
- **C7:** источник оплат абонплаты на продукт — настройка `Product.payment_source` (`elqr` | `product` | `manual`), по умолчанию `product` (пока Элкюар не подключён); остальные источники только сверяют.

## 1. Spec issues (ranked)

### Critical: money or data breaks

| # | Issue | Where | Proposed resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Mixed currencies in one ledger.** P3 puts franchise contracts, charges and payments in USD. Withholding offsets (5Г) and `withhold` charges come from KGS sales. `fee_remaining = fee_total − Σpayments(contract)` and `balance = ΣCharge − ΣPayment` (billing/services.py) add KGS and USD as plain numbers. Example: a KGS 50 000 offset would "pay off" a $1 000 contract. | plan P3, 5Г, billing/services.py `finance_map`, clients/services.py `fee_remaining` | Add `currency` to Charge, FranchiseePayment, CommissionPayout. Convert every KGS offset into contract currency at the `ExchangeRate` on the sale date, and store `amount`, `amount_contract_ccy`, `rate`. Report balances per currency. Never sum USD and KGS without a rate. |
| C2 | **Overdue is masked by offsets.** `overdue = Σcharges(due<today) − Σ ALL payments`. Offsets that repay the no-due "from sales" part also cancel overdue installments and overdue `withhold` charges. Example: a setup sale on day −40 creates a `withhold` charge of 1 500 due day −10. Its own offset is also 1 500, so overdue = 0 even though nothing was paid. This feeds rating discipline, the "Требует внимания" list and NBS §16 installment debt. | TZ §5 rule, 5Г, billing/services.py | Allocate payments to charges: offsets go only to the sales part; bank/cash go FIFO by due date. Then overdue = Σ unpaid amounts of charges with due < today. Test: `test_specdep_04`. |
| C3 | **Recruiter $150 counts money that did not come in.** P3 counts "удержания из продаж" as money received. For setup/equipment, the withheld amount stays with the franchisee: it becomes a new `withhold` Charge, and the offset is booked at the same moment. NBS has received nothing, which violates NBS §5.3 ("пока NBS не получила деньги — начисления нет"). When the franchisee later pays that charge by bank, the same money counts a second time. | plan P3 | Count only (a) confirmed cash/bank payments linked to franchise-contract charges and (b) subscription-withholding offsets, because the client paid NBS directly. Exclude setup/equipment offsets. |
| C4 | **Recruiter reward has no confirmation state and no reallocation rule.** NBS says "после подтверждения платежа", but FranchiseePayment has no status. If an allocated payment is cancelled, the plan only "reduces accrued". It does not re-run FIFO over the remaining payments, so the reward can stay below $150 while $475 was received. The plan also allows the same finance user to verify and pay. | plan P3 | Add `status` (confirmed/cancelled) to FranchiseePayment. Recompute the allocation FIFO over confirmed payments on every change. Keep `paid` immutable and post a correction instead. Optionally require `verified_by ≠ paid_by`. Test: `test_specdep_ac3_cancel_payment_reallocates_fifo`. |
| C5 | **The Branch migration stops all commissions.** P5 attaches every ClientProductAccount to a new main branch, and the recipient is `implementer_partner` only when it equals `responsible_partner`. The plan never says to fill these fields during migration. With null fields, no partner qualifies. | plan P5 | In the migration, set seller, implementer and responsible on the main branch to `client.franchisee`. Add a test that a payment after migration still accrues 20%. |
| C6 | **Two sources of truth for subscription money and commission.** P9 adds `Payment` and P8 adds `CommissionAccrual`/`Payout`. The existing ClientSale.commission_amount/franchisee_income, CommissionPayout(offset) and `commission_due = Σcommission_amount − ΣCommissionPayout` stay in place. The 5Б product flow "writes here as well". Unless one is retired, reports and dashboards (revenue, plan fact, rating) will double count, and commission_due will diverge from the accruals. | plan P8/P9 vs clients/reports.py, dashboard/services.py, planning/services.py | Make Payment plus CommissionAccrual canonical for subscriptions. Derive ClientSale(subscription/renewal) from Payment 1:1 (FK, never a second insert), or drop it. Compute `commission_due` from accruals: Σ(amount − withheld) − Σpaid payouts. Migrate CommissionPayout(offset) into `accrual.withheld_amount`. |
| C7 | **Duplicate payments from Elqr and the product.** NBS §10.1/§15 makes Elqr the payment source. The product sends payment status only "если подтверждена через централизованный контур". The plan keeps 5Б `payment.succeeded` and also adds the Elqr webhook and bank import. These carry different external IDs, so the same client payment can arrive twice and earn commission twice. | plan P9 | Pick one authoritative source per product (`Product.payment_source = elqr|product|manual`). Other sources only reconcile and must not create a Payment. Bank import must match against existing Elqr operations by operation ID and account. |
| C8 | **Recipient changes apply retroactively.** Branch reassignment (P5) and closure (P5) overwrite `responsible_partner`. Accruals for late payments, refunds and re-processing would then go to the new partner, even when `paid_at` predates the change. | plan P5 | Choose the recipient as of `paid_at` from `BranchAssignmentHistory`, which needs an `effective_from` field. Book reversals to the partner of the original accrual. |
| C9 | **Isolation breaks after a branch is reassigned.** Visibility today is scoped by `client.franchisee` (core/viewsets.py). After branch 2 moves to partner Y, Y cannot see the branch's client, while X still sees Y's payments and accruals. This violates NBS §3 ("не видит ... платежи других франчайзи"). | plan P5 vs core/viewsets.py | Scope branch, subscription, payment and accrual data by the branch's responsible or implementer partner. Scope client data by "has at least one branch I serve" and show only those branches. Test: AC8. |
| C10 | **OnAI 20 000 split conflicts with "Установка — 100%".** The seeded rule gives the franchisee 100% of 20 000 = 20 000, and the implementer also gets 8 000, so 28 000 is paid out of 20 000. | NBS §9.2, plan P7 vs 5Д seed | A SetupSplitRule overrides CommissionRule for that product. Franchisee income = `partner_amount` (12 000), and withholding is computed on 12 000. Define what happens when setup is sold below 20 000: pro-rata or fixed 8 000 to the implementer. |
| C11 | **A franchisee-specific rule beats tier.** Rule weights from 5Д give franchisee +4. The planned `partner_tier` and `lead_source` dimensions have no weights. A seeded "OZOK Бишкек Юг — 30%" rule therefore keeps 30% after a downgrade to Standard (NBS §11.2) and overrides the NBS-lead adjustment (§11.3). | 5Д §3 + plan P4/P8 | Calculate in two steps. First the base rate from product × tier (or an explicit franchisee override that must itself carry `partner_tier`). Then apply the NBS-lead adjustment as a separate, stored component (`adjustment_rule`, `adjustment_amount`) so "удержано/причина" is visible (§11.1). Weights for tier and lead_source must be defined. Drop the duplicate `effective_from` and keep `valid_from`. |
| C12 | **Hybrid "from sales" part has no Charge.** 5А created a no-due `fee` charge for per_sale. 5Д lists only upfront and installment charges. Without a charge for the remainder, the offsets push the balance negative. | 5Д §2 | Create `fee` with `due_date = null` for the remainder. Test: `test_specdep_01`. |

### High

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Tier effective date versus multi-month prepayment. A 12-month payment made while Growth is active earns 30% for the whole year, even if the partner is downgraded the next month. Late imports use the import date instead of the bank operation date. | Rate is taken at `paid_at`, which is the operation date, never the import time. Optionally split by covered month (`period_from..period_to`). NBS must decide (§21.5). |
| H2 | Suspended status: NBS says "Нет до решения NBS". The plan creates 0 accruals, so restoring the partner cannot release the money. | Create accruals with status `on_hold`. NBS decides to release or cancel with a reason. |
| H3 | Closure leaves open money questions: accrued-unpaid commissions, outstanding franchise installments, withholding and the recruiter reward. "Уходят новому ответственному" contradicts NBS §7.2.4 (no commission to whoever did not implement). Refunds after closure of pre-closure payments go to a closed partner. | Pre-closure accruals stay payable after verification. Post-closure payments accrue to NBS (no partner) unless they are formally reassigned with an effective date. Open franchise debt stays in the partner ledger. Closing a partner closes its contracts. |
| H4 | The NBS-lead link for subscription payments is undefined. Payment → branch → client → `Client.lead` is never specified. Branch 2 would inherit the NBS-lead adjustment forever. | Store `lead`/`lead_source` on Branch, copied from the deal. The adjustment applies only to branches created from that lead. |
| H5 | CommissionAccrual statuses mix the lifecycle with `withheld`, and P8 sets the original to `reversed` even after it is paid, which destroys the paid history. | Keep the original status (paid stays paid). The reversal is a negative accrual netted into the next payout. Carry a negative payout balance forward, never pay a negative amount. `withheld_amount` stays a number, not a status. |
| H6 | Product code rename (`market` → `nur_retail` …) has no migration. Integration payloads, `Lead.product` strings, `package:<id>` and client filters all use codes. | Add a data migration that renames codes in place (same IDs, same API keys) and rewrites `Lead.product`. Alternatively keep the old codes and add the new ones as `external_code`. |
| H7 | Deleting financial data is forbidden (NBS §18), but the current API deletes ClientSale and cascades the offset payments and withhold charges. Cancel semantics for a withhold charge that was already paid are undefined. | Use `cancel/` with a reason. Reverse by negative entries. If the charge is already paid, leave a credit (negative balance). |
| H8 | Funnel "1st/2nd/3rd paid sale" (P2) has no source entity (ClientDeal `paid`? a subscription payment?), and refunding a counted sale is not handled. | Count ClientDeal status `paid`, without regressing the stage on refund (record it in history). |
| H9 | `recruited_by` is restricted to the recruiter role. NBS says "Барсбек, Мирлан или иной сотрудник", and it is unclear whether another employee also earns $150. | Allow any NBS user. The reward is created only if the user has the recruiter role or a `recruiter_reward_enabled` flag. |
| H10 | Who receives commission when the original partner implemented branch 2 but NBS changed `responsible_partner` for support reasons only? The plan's "иначе — правило ниже" never names a recipient. | Implementer receives it while also responsible. Otherwise NBS (no accrual), with a reason code. |

### Medium / Low
- M1. Minimum price applies only to monthly tariffs (P6). NBS §8 and §21.3 also cover setup, training and equipment. Extend `min_price` to those.
- M2. The `assigned` lead status is removed (P4), and with it the 24 h overdue rule. The NBS §6.2.5 reassignment trigger needs a defined SLA, such as `next_contact_at` passed or no activity for N hours.
- M3. Rounding is not specified anywhere for accruals, withholding, FX or proportional refunds. Use half-up to 0.01 at each stored line. The final refund or reversal takes the exact remainder. Installments use ROUND_DOWN, and the last one takes the kopecks (already implemented).
- M4. Tariff price history: `valid_to` should be inclusive and equal to `new.valid_from − 1`. Reject a `valid_from` at or before the current record's `valid_from`, because that creates an overlap. If the event's `list_amount` differs from the computed one, store the event value and log a warning. Clamp `discount_amount` at 0.
- M5. Rule validation needs these checks: percent or fixed must be set; `fixed_per=month` only for subscriptions; the tariff must belong to the rule's product; no tariff on setup/equipment rules. Decide whether income may exceed the paid amount.
- M6. TZ.md §11 acceptance #3/#4 ("продажа → начисление") and §7 (bundle ×0.70) are obsolete. §10 seed uses `license`/`service`. Replace them with NBS §20.
- M7. Recruiter can see partner payments (plan P3). NBS §3 only grants "статус своей выплаты". Least privilege (§18): show totals only.
- M8. `partners/{id}/close/` (P5) and the `franchisees/` resource (P1) use different paths.
- M9. Rating: Standard partners get a 0 or neutral marketing block. Choose neutral 50% by default so Standard partners are not penalised for not being Growth.
- M10. Lead "Хочу подключить" (5В) creates a Lead with `interest=franchise` and `franchisee` set to the requester, so the requester can `take`/`win` its own request. Hide these leads from the franchisee's actions.
